# SIH Dashboard APP Structure

This document outlines the product specifications, architecture, and feature breakdown for the KIKI Health Dashboard.

## Key Modules
1. **Onboarding Flow**: 3-step personalized setup (Personal Details, Activity/Location, Daily Goals).
2. **Dashboard**: Daily rings, metric cards, and heart rate trends toggle (graph/summary).
3. **Weather & Heatwave Advisory**: IMD heatwave protocols, AQI, and local alerts.
4. **Sleep Monitoring**: 7D/14D duration trends and sleep stages.
5. **Safety Center**: Fall detection alerts and emergency triggers.
6. **Heart Rate Alerts**: Clinical symptom assessments for high and low HR.
7. **Profile & Goals**: User settings, baseline customization, and account reset.
