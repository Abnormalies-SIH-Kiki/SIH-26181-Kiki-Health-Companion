SHOE UPDATE — SPEAKER WIRE EXIT

Print ONLY PRINT_SHOE_SPEAKER_EXIT.stl, one copy, at 100% scale.
Use the existing 0.16 mm Health Companion profile, supports off.
The supplied orientation has the flat skin-facing surface on the bed.
The open strap and wire channels face upward.

This replaces the original shoe. Reuse the already printed tray, keeper,
adapter, pins and shims. Existing original files and full plate are unchanged;
the old full plate does NOT contain this revised shoe.

Assembly:
1. Seat the MAX30102 in its existing pocket.
2. Orient the C-shaped keeper with its open side facing the new narrow channel
   beside the sensor pocket.
3. Bring the speaker wires down through the tray's existing sensor access
   opening. Route them over the back of the sensor PCB, through the keeper's
   open side, into the new channel, and out at the nearby short end of the shoe.
   Keep wires clear of the optical face and keeper bearing surfaces.
4. Lay the wires fully inside the channel before fastening the tray.

Channel nominal dimensions: 5 mm wide, 3 mm deep, with 2 mm of plastic below.
The tray closes the top of the channel. No extra printed cover is required.
Checked CAD clearance for two insulated wires each 1.4 mm in outside diameter.
This is a geometry check, not a physical wiring/fit test or connector clearance.
The channel clears the strap tunnel and four locking sockets.
